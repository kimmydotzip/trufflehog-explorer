Filters

Pattern syntax
  • Separate patterns with commas: AWS, Azure* matches either.
  • * any characters, ? one character. Wildcard patterns match the whole value: *.lock.
  • Text without wildcards matches anywhere: terraform.
  • /regex/ for regular expressions: /^AKIA/.
  • Prefix ! to exclude: !*.lock, !*package.json.
  • Everything is case-insensitive.
